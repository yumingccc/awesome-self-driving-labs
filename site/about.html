About Awesome Self-Driving Labs

A paper index for AI methods applied to real or simulated scientific experiments.

What we include

We include papers in which AI is used to design, run, simulate, analyze, or evaluate scientific experiments. Topics include self-driving labs, lab robotics, biological protocols, computational research agents, and scientific simulators.

CORE SDL

Autonomous systems that connect experimental decisions to execution and measurements; foundational reviews of those systems.

ENABLING TECHNOLOGY

AI methods and agents evaluated in laboratory tasks or scientific computational experiments, including simulation-based benchmarks.

EXCLUDED

Household robotics without experimental tasks; automation with no identifiable AI component; general-purpose AI not evaluated on scientific experimentation.

Publications and dates

We include relevant work from journals, research conferences, and arXiv. Every entry must use AI in a scientific experimental setting, including simulated experiments. Code and project links are shown only when a paper-specific resource can be identified.

A conference entry displays the date of its latest arXiv version alongside the conference name, so the displayed date is not the conference presentation date. Preprint and journal versions of the same work appear as one entry, dated by whichever version is newest.

Research value stars

Stars are editorial reading recommendations, not citation counts or measures of scientific quality.

★★★

Foundational work or a particularly informative system, method, or benchmark.

★★☆

A relevant contribution worth reading for its approach or application.

★☆☆

Useful additional reading on a narrower question.
